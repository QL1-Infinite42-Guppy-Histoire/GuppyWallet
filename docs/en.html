GuppyWallet

Part of Guppy's Guide（グッピーズガイド） — Don't Panic — a hitchhiker's toolbox for 42 Chain & QL1

GuppyWallet is a wallet made for 42 Chain and QL1. Keep your coins, send and receive, swap, and more — all in plain words, right from your phone.

⚠️ Before you download

This app is still in development. Unexpected bugs may cause a loss of assets. Please use it at your own risk — and do not use it to store assets you cannot afford to lose.

📥 Download (APK)

v0.4.0 (in development) · Android · ~108 MB

What it does

  • See your coins on 42 Chain and QL1, in one place
  • Send and receive — with a clear check screen before anything moves
  • Swap coins inside the app
  • Put coins to work — liquidity, staking and more
  • Move coins between chains with the built-in bridge

Good to know

Still in development. Unexpected bugs may cause a loss of assets. Please use it at your own risk — and do not use it to store assets you cannot afford to lose. Start with a small amount first.

Free and open source. Everything is open under the MIT license — anyone can read the source code: source code on GitHub.
